HR Analytics Dashboard - Performance & Engagement
This repository features a Business Intelligence project focused on analyzing Human Resources Key Performance Indicators (KPIs). The goal is to provide data-driven insights into employee engagement, satisfaction, and performance to support strategic HR decision-making.

📊 Dashboard Overview
The dashboard analyzes a dataset of 387 employees, transforming raw data into actionable visuals that track organizational health through both quantitative and qualitative metrics.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):
Total Employees: 387

Average Engagement Score: 2.90

Average Satisfaction Score: 3.12

📈 Key Analyses
Engagement by Department: A percentage comparison of engagement levels across various departments, such as Admin Offices, IT/IS, and Production.

Satisfaction by Department Type: Identification of satisfaction gaps, highlighting areas that may require management attention (e.g., Admin Offices at 40%).

Work-Life Balance Trend: A monthly timeline tracking employee perception of work-life balance from August 2022 to August 2023.

Performance Score Distribution: A donut chart detailing performance ratings (Fully Meets, Exceeds, Needs Improvement, and PIP).

Dynamic Filtering: Ability to segment data by Employee Status (Active, Terminated, Leave of Absence, etc.).
